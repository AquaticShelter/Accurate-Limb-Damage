# Limb Damage

Limb damage like Call of Duty: hit the leg and it hurts less than the chest. Every body part has its own damage
number, TACZ guns can have their own numbers per gun type, and when you die you get a recap of who hit you, with
what, and where. There's also a training dummy you can dress up and shoot.

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10+, Java 17. Needs YACL 3.6+. TACZ and Accurate Hitboxes are optional.
